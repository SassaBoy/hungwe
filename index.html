NAMIBIA'S ENERGY BACKBONE

Horizon Fuels & Logistics is a wholly Namibian-owned petroleum company headquartered in Windhoek. Since 2016, we've been the trusted energy partner for industries, governments and logistics operators across Southern Africa.
