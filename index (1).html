Hungwe Civil Engineering & Construction is a proudly 100% Namibian-owned company headquartered at Ausspannplatz, Windhoek. We deliver world-class engineering from complex civil infrastructure to bespoke home renovations.

With over 20 years of combined expertise, our team of engineers, project managers, and skilled tradespeople have successfully completed projects for government institutions, corporations, and private clients across Namibia — including institutional bids with NUST.
